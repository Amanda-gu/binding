Darkroom

Type design is often romanticized as an art—a free-flowing expression of form and beauty. This is only half the story. Beneath the surface of every letterform lies a scaffold of engineering. Precise calculations. Material constraints. Systematic logic. A typeface is not merely drawn; it is architected, engineered, and programmed to function under real-world conditions. This collection brings together three foundational texts that dismantle the myth of the font as a static artwork.
